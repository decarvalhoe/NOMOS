# Code Of Conduct

Nomos contributors are expected to collaborate professionally and keep technical discussion focused on evidence, reproducibility, and project outcomes.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, respectful, and specific.
- Challenge claims with evidence.
- Separate facts, assumptions, and opinions.
- Preserve confidentiality of private corpora, customer material, licensed references, and security information.
- Correct overclaims when found.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory conduct.
- Publishing private, licensed, or customer-owned material without permission.
- Misrepresenting validation, security, compliance, or evidence status.
- Hiding known blockers in release or customer-facing documentation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, restrict repository access, or block contributions that violate this policy.
