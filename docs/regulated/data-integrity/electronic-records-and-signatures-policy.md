## Purpose

Define the current boundary for electronic records and electronic signatures in Nomos.

## Current Boundary

Nomos may generate evidence records. Nomos does not yet provide a validated electronic signature system.

## Required Before Any Part 11-Like Claim

- intended-use assessment;
- record inventory;
- system validation evidence;
- accurate human-readable and electronic record export;
- retention and retrieval controls;
- access restriction;
- secure computer-generated audit trails;
- authority checks;
- operational checks where applicable;
- training records;
- written accountability policies;
- documentation change controls;
- signature meaning and signature-to-record binding if signatures are implemented.
