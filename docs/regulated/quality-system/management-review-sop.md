## Purpose

Define periodic review of Nomos quality, compliance posture, risk, evidence and customer readiness.

## Required Inputs

- open deviations and CAPA;
- validation status;
- release evidence status;
- audit findings;
- customer findings;
- security/vulnerability status;
- AI/RAG eval status;
- supplier/subprocessor status;
- training completion;
- effectiveness of previous actions;
- public claim boundary.

## Required Outputs

- decisions;
- action owners;
- due dates;
- resource needs;
- accepted risks;
- blocked claims;
- next review date.
