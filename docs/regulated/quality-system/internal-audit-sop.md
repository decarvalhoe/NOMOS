## Purpose

Define how Nomos performs internal audits of its regulated-by-design system.

## Audit Scope

Internal audits may cover:

- reference register and control matrix;
- document and record control;
- training records;
- validation pack;
- data integrity and audit trail;
- secure SDLC and supply chain;
- AI/RAG governance;
- release bundles;
- deviations/CAPA;
- customer integration evidence.

## Conduct

Auditors must be independent of the work audited where practical. If independence is not possible, record the limitation and require management review.

Every audit produces:

- audit plan;
- audit checklist;
- evidence sampled;
- findings;
- severity;
- CAPA links;
- audit report;
- follow-up and closure evidence.
