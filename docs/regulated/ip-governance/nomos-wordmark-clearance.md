# NOMOS Wordmark Clearance Note

Status: `clearance_required_before_public_claim`

This note is not legal advice. It is a product governance record that blocks public trademark-style claims until counsel or a qualified trademark reviewer completes clearance.

## Proposed Mark

The working distinctive wordmark is `NOMOS Canonical Knowledge Mesh`.

Standalone `NOMOS` is treated as crowded for public positioning because it is a common classical/legal term and appears across legal, watch, software, and AI-adjacent uses. Public materials may use NOMOS descriptively for the repository/project only when they avoid registration, exclusivity, or freedom-to-use claims.

This is a crowding signal, not a legal conclusion. The project must not claim that `NOMOS`, `NOMOS Canonical Knowledge Mesh`, or any abbreviation is available, registrable, registered, or non-infringing until a formal clearance memo exists.

## Publication Controls

Allowed before clearance:

- factual repository references such as "the NOMOS project";
- internal engineering branch names and issue names;
- descriptive phrases explaining canonical-first source traceability.

Blocked before clearance:

- registered trademark symbols;
- claims that the mark is cleared, protected, unique, defensible, or exclusive;
- paid advertising or customer-facing launch copy centered on the mark;
- public claims that omit the `clearance_required_before_public_claim` status.

## Clearance Requirements

Before a public launch or customer-facing brand claim, record:

- live screenshots or exports from WIPO Global Brand Database, EUIPO eSearch, and USPTO Trademark Search;
- goods and services mapping by jurisdiction and class;
- conflict analysis for `NOMOS`, `NOMOS AI`, `NOMOS Canonical Knowledge Mesh`, and likely abbreviations;
- counsel or qualified trademark-review approval;
- a decision record updating this status.
